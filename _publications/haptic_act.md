Robotic manipulation of delicate objects requires compliant control that mirrors human intuition. Haptic-ACT bridges human demonstration and compliant robotic manipulation through an immersive VR teleoperation interface with haptic feedback, enabling the robot to learn contact-rich manipulation skills from intuitive human demonstrations.
